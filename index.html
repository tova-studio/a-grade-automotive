Services

What does A Grade Automotive do?

A Grade Automotive are mechanics. We repair cars for Christchurch drivers from our workshop on Ferry Road, and every job starts with a quick phone call.

I.

Mechanical work

When your car doesn’t feel, sound or drive the way it should, it’s time to talk to a mechanic. Ring the workshop, describe what you’ve noticed, and arrange a time to bring it in to Ferry Road.

II.

Car repair

Car repair is the heart of what we do. Phone 03 384 4030 to explain what has happened to your vehicle and to discuss getting it repaired at our Christchurch workshop.

III.

Not sure what your car needs?

You don’t need to know the technical name for a problem. Tell us what you’re experiencing over the phone and ask whether A Grade Automotive can help. It’s the quickest way to get an answer.

About

Who are A Grade Automotive?

A Grade Automotive is a Christchurch mechanic and car repair business based at 1030 Ferry Road. We keep things straightforward: a local workshop that’s easy to find, a phone number you can call, and a plain-English conversation about your car.

If you live or work around Christchurch and your vehicle needs a mechanic’s attention, call 03 384 4030. We’ll talk about what your car needs and when you can bring it to the workshop.

Booking

How do I book my car in?

Booking your car in with A Grade Automotive takes three simple steps.

  1. 01

    Call the workshop

    Phone 03 384 4030. Calling is the quickest way to reach us and arrange a time that suits you.

  2. 02

    What should I have ready when I call?

    Have your car’s make, model and registration handy, and describe what you’ve noticed, such as noises, warning lights or changes in how it drives.

  3. 03

    Bring it to Ferry Road

    Bring your car to 1030 Ferry Road, Christchurch, at the time you’ve arranged with us.
